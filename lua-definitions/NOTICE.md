# Distribution scope and provenance

This repository is the public Macro Handler LuaLS editor-support package.
The files are intended for local IDE support while authoring Macro Handler macros.
The declarations are not executable implementations of the Android APIs.

Publication of this repository does not publish or license Macro Handler's Android runtime,
backend, application assets, private source history or other product components. Downloading
definitions does not provide premium entitlements or change application terms.

No LuaJ, LuaLS, OpenCV or native Lua implementation/binary is bundled. The declarations were
prepared from Macro Handler bindings and pinned LuaJ interfaces; annotation conventions
follow LuaLS's public documentation. Upstream interface reference links are recorded in
`metadata.json` and the audit. Product names and links identify their respective projects.

For distribution or licensing questions, contact [Macro Handler support](https://macrohandler.com/support).
The app and product documentation are available at [https://macrohandler.com](https://macrohandler.com) and
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.macrohandler.app).
